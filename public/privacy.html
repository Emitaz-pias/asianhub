Privacy Policy

Asian Hub · Last updated: October 4, 2026

Information we collect

When you submit the application form, it collects the details shown in that form, such as your name, country, email address, phone number, preferred contact method, contact handle, and submission time. Please provide only information you are comfortable sharing for this inquiry.

How information is used

Asian Hub uses submitted details to review your inquiry and contact you about your application. Do not submit passwords, one-time codes, account PINs, bank details, or payment credentials.

Storage and service providers

Form submissions are sent through SheetDB and stored in the Google Sheet connected to the site's SheetDB API. Google and SheetDB process data under their own terms and privacy practices. Access to the connected Sheet and API should be limited to authorized site operators and configured only for the functions the form requires.

Retention and security

The site operator should retain application details only as long as needed to handle inquiries and meet applicable obligations, then delete them securely. No online transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices and requests

You can choose not to submit the form. To ask about, correct, or request deletion of information you submitted, use the form on the Asian Hub home page and clearly state that your message is a privacy request. Include enough information for the operator to identify your submission, but do not send account credentials or payment information.

External links and updates

This website may link to third-party services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. This notice may be updated; the date above shows when it was last revised.

This is a general privacy notice, not legal advice. Before launch, the site operator should confirm the actual data flow, retention period, privacy contact process, and legal requirements for the site's audience.
