Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026.

Who handles the data

The website is administered under the Asian Hub community name by the people who operate this site. Asian Hub is a community name, not a separately identified registered company. Send privacy requests through the contact form.

What the form collects and why

The form asks for your email address and a short message so the site operators can respond to your inquiry or community interest. It also records your required consent and submission time. Do not include passwords, identity documents, payment details, or other sensitive information.

Where information goes

When you submit the form, the site sends these fields to SheetDB, which writes them to the Google Sheet connected to the site's API. SheetDB processes the request; the Google Sheet stores the submission. People authorized to access that SheetDB API account or Google Sheet may be able to access the submissions. The site does not need a name, phone number, country, or social-media handle to reply.

Use, sharing, and retention

The site operators use the message only to respond to the inquiry, review community interest, or handle a report. They do not sell form messages or use them for advertising. The operators review the Google Sheet, delete messages after the inquiry is handled, and remove any remaining message no later than 12 months after receipt. SheetDB and Google may process data under their own service terms and privacy policies.

Advertising measurement

The Google Ads tag AW-18529286301 is present on the site and starts with advertising storage, user data, and personalization consent denied. In this denied state, Google may receive limited cookieless measurement pings and technical information such as browser and device data and page activity. If you select Allow in the site's advertising measurement prompt, the site updates consent to allow Google Ads advertising storage and measurement cookies. If you select Reject, those remain denied. Email addresses and message contents from the contact form are not sent to Google Ads. Your choice is stored in this browser; use Cookie settings on the home page to change it.

Your choices

You may ask the site operators to provide, correct, or delete a message by sending a new request through the contact form. The site is intended for people in all countries except Bangladesh. Privacy rights and requirements vary by location; this policy does not claim a particular legal status or compliance certification.

Changes

This policy will be updated if the form fields, reasons for use, service providers, or data practices change.

Read the SheetDB privacy policy and Google privacy policy for their handling of data.
