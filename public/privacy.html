Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026.

Who handles the data

The website is administered under the Asian Hub community name by the people who operate this site. Asian Hub is a community name, not a separately identified registered company. Send privacy requests through the contact form.

What the form collects and why

The form asks for your email address and a short message so the site operators can respond to your inquiry or community interest. It also records your required consent. Do not include passwords, identity documents, payment details, or other sensitive information.

Form provider and access

The form is prepared to use Netlify Forms, the site's hosting provider. When Netlify Forms detection is enabled for this site and the updated deployment is live, Netlify receives and stores submissions in the site's Netlify dashboard. Netlify may process technical submission data such as time, IP address, and spam-filter signals to provide and protect the service. People with authorized access to the site's Netlify account can view submissions. Netlify's handling is also subject to its Privacy Policy.

Use, sharing, and retention

The site operators use the message only to respond to the inquiry, review community interest, or handle a report. They do not sell form messages or use them for advertising. The site operators review submissions in the Netlify dashboard, delete messages after the inquiry is handled, and remove any remaining message no later than 12 months after receipt. Netlify may retain technical records under its own retention practices.

Analytics and tracking

This site does not currently load Google Analytics, Google Ads conversion tracking, or other advertising analytics. Form values are not placed in URLs or tracking events. The hosting provider may process technical request information needed to operate the website.

Your choices

You may ask the site operators to provide, correct, or delete a message by sending a new request through the contact form. The site is intended for people in all countries except Bangladesh. Privacy rights and requirements vary by location; this policy does not claim a particular legal status or compliance certification.

Changes

This policy will be updated if the form fields, reasons for use, service providers, or data practices change.
