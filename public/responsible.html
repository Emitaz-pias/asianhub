Responsible Play

Asian Hub · Information for visitors

Keep play in balance

Games and sports-related activities should remain entertainment. Participation may involve financial risk, and losses are possible. Never spend money you need for essentials, borrow to participate, or treat play as a way to earn a reliable income.

Set limits and take breaks

  • Set a time and spending limit before you start, and stop when you reach it.
  • Do not chase losses or participate when upset, under pressure, or impaired.
  • Take regular breaks and keep other activities and responsibilities in balance.
  • Use age-appropriate restrictions and keep minors away from gambling services.

Get support

If participation is causing stress, debt, or problems with family or work, stop and talk with someone you trust. Consider contacting a qualified support service available in your country. Asian Hub does not provide counselling or operate gambling services.

Check local rules

Minimum age, availability, and laws differ by location. You are responsible for confirming local requirements before using any third-party service referenced on this site.
