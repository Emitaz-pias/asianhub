Community Rules

Keep discussion useful and respectful

These rules apply to messages sent through this website and any Asian Hub discussion space that may be opened later. This site currently has no public forum or chat.

1. Be respectful

Discuss ideas without harassment, threats, hate speech, insults, or discrimination. Disagree with claims, not a person's right to participate.

2. Stay on topic

Keep discussion about sports, games, match analysis, and prediction culture. Do not spam, impersonate others, or post unsolicited promotions.

3. Be clear about claims

Separate fact, opinion, and prediction. Give a reliable source when practical. Do not present an uncertain prediction as a guaranteed result.

4. Protect privacy

Do not publish another person's contact details, private messages, passwords, identity documents, or financial information.

5. No financial solicitation

Do not use Asian Hub to solicit money, accept wagers, sell betting access, recruit agents or affiliates, promise income, or collect account credentials.

6. Moderation

Website messages are reviewed by the people administering Asian Hub. Messages that breach these rules may be deleted and the sender may not receive a reply. No member discussion area is currently live.

7. Report a concern

Use the contact form to report a concern. Send only the information needed to explain it; do not include sensitive personal or financial information.
